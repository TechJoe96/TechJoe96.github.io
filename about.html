About Me

🌏 My Journey

I grew up in Seoul, Korea, surrounded by the fast-paced energy of one of the world's most tech-driven cities. In 2022, I made the leap across the Pacific to Chicago, where I pursued a dual degree in Computer Engineering at Illinois Institute of Technology. Two years later, I moved to New York City to begin my M.S. at NYU Tandon — and I've been thriving in the city's electric energy ever since.

My academic passion lies at the intersection of AI/ML engineering and VLSI/chip design. I believe the next frontier isn't just about building bigger models — it's about making them leaner, faster, and more accessible. Whether it's building multimodal AI systems or designing chip-level hardware on FPGA/ASIC platforms, I thrive on solving problems at both ends of the stack.

🤝 Beyond the Lab

Engineering is what I do, but connecting with people is who I am. At Illinois Tech, I founded the Korean Student Association from scratch — rallying students, organizing cultural events, and building a community that didn't exist before. I also joined Triangle Fraternity, where I learned as much about leadership and teamwork as I did about engineering.

Whether it's rallying a student org, collaborating on a group project, or just grabbing coffee with a new friend, I thrive on bringing people together and making things happen.
